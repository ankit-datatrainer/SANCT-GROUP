02 / 08 01 · REQUIREMENTS

Start with the radio system.

Plan the antenna alongside the enclosure, PCB and radio so the assembled product can meet its RF targets.

01

Antenna technologies

Select bands and radio technologies for the application, from cellular and sub-6 GHz or mmWave 5G to Wi-Fi, BLE, GNSS, satellite and ISM links.

02

System architecture

Translate product requirements into frequency, polarization, coverage and compliance targets. Consider EMI, EMC and certification needs early.

03 / 08 02 · DESIGN

Geometry shapes the signal.

Compare monopole, dipole, loop, helical, patch, slot and inverted-F designs against available space, gain and radiation-pattern requirements.

Bands and form factor
Identify what must fit and where it must operate.
Model and compare
Compare concepts with electromagnetic models such as HFSS or CST.

04 / 08 03 · INTEGRATION

Design for the assembled device.

Review PCB materials, stack-up, feed routing and the enclosure. Evaluate off-the-shelf antenna options, matching and isolation when radios share the same device.

05 / 08 04 · VERIFICATION

Measure the RF performance.

Define how to verify gain, efficiency, polarization, return loss and VSWR. For cellular products, consider over-the-air TRP and TIS measurements.

06 / 08 PERFORMANCE OPTIMIZATION

Find the source of the loss.

Use electromagnetic simulation and prototype measurements to identify loss, detuning and coupling. Refine transmission lines, the matching network or filtering as needed.
