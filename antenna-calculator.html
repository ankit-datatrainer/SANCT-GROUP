03 / 08 THE FORMULA

Wavelength = c ÷ f.

Divide the speed of light in vacuum (299,792,458 m/s) by frequency in hertz. The calculator displays the result in millimetres.

04 / 08 READING THE RESULTS

One wave. Three lengths.

Full wavelength
The distance covered by one complete cycle in free space.
Half-wave
One half of the full wavelength.
Quarter-wave
One quarter of the full wavelength.

07 / 08 FREQUENCY UNITS

MHz or GHz?

How do the units relate?

1 GHz is 1,000 MHz. Enter 2.4 GHz or 2,400 MHz to calculate the same wavelength.

What happens as frequency rises?

The wavelength gets shorter because it is inversely proportional to frequency.
